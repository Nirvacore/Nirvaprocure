# 01 — Vision Overview

## Mission

Build the procurement operating system for Thailand and ASEAN — a single platform that unifies marketplace buying, ERP workflows, and AI-driven procurement intelligence.

## What NIRVAPROCURE Is

A composite layer that combines four traditionally separate systems:

- **Marketplace** — direct integration with Shopee, Lazada, Alibaba, Makro, and supplier networks
- **ERP** — finance, inventory, HR, and operations workflow
- **AI** — price comparison, supplier risk, fraud detection, forecasting
- **Procurement Layer** — requisitions, approvals, POs, contracts, and government TOR automation

## Target Customers

- **SMEs** — small and medium businesses that buy from marketplaces but lack approval discipline and accounting integration
- **Mid-market** — companies with ERP fragments and manual procurement workflows
- **Enterprise** — organizations needing ISO-grade approval flows, audit trails, and multi-entity consolidation
- **Government suppliers** — vendors and buyers handling TOR (Terms of Reference) processes

## Differentiators

- **Marketplace-native procurement** — most ERPs treat marketplace buying as an afterthought; NIRVAPROCURE makes it first-class.
- **Thailand/ASEAN-first** — LINE notifications, Thai accounting compliance, Makro/Lazada/Shopee deep integration, government TOR workflows.
- **AI-augmented decisions** — every purchase request can be enriched with price comparison, supplier risk, and anomaly detection.
- **Workflow-driven approval** — ISO-compatible approval chains usable by both small teams and large enterprises.
